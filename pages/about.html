barkada - a Filipino word that means "a group of friends"

Expenses are not always shared equally.

Whether you have a party or a simple dinner with your barkada, there will be shared expenses. Sometimes, these expenses are not shared equally. You may have one friend who took the bus and therefore will not contribute to gas expenses, or a friend who's allergic to cake and will not be paying for dessert expenses. On the other hand, everyone might just want to contribute to everything equally.

Whatever your needs are, Barkada Bills can help.

With this expense splitter, you can add the members of your group, add your expenses, and indicate who contributes to which expense. This tool will provide the total contribution of each barkada member, taking into account the expenses they are contributing to. You won't need to go back and forth with calculations anymore as this app makes it easier for you and your barkada!

This is a CS50x final project by Iam Rasendi Saldua. I hope this can be of use to someone out there!
